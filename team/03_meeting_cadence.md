# Meeting Cadence

- Monday: workstream standups, 30 min each.
- Wednesday: systems integration, 60 min.
- Alternate Fridays: technical deep dive or calculation review, 60 min.
- Final Friday of each month: gate review, 90 min.

Every meeting should end with named owners, due dates, and issue links. Slides are not the system of record; GitHub issues and reviewed files are.
