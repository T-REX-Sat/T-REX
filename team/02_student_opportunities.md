# Brown Student Opportunities

## Credit

Structure bounded work packages as independent study or thesis/capstone work, subject to faculty sponsorship and departmental rules.

## Funding

Use Brown UTRA/SPRINT mechanisms for faculty-student research collaborations. Prepare project descriptions early enough for the next published cycle rather than assuming a deadline.

## Scholarly outputs

- mission concept or methods paper;
- technical note on sensitivity, timing, orbit, or data architecture;
- posters at Brown research symposia and relevant conferences;
- conference abstracts and stakeholder presentations.

## Technical ownership

Students may own hardware breadboards, analysis software, simulations, documentation, verification tests, and proposal sections. Each work package should have a mentor, acceptance test, and publication/disclosure plan.

## Intellectual property

Potentially patentable concepts should be discussed with Brown's technology-transfer office before public disclosure. Authorship and inventorship are different and should be handled explicitly.
