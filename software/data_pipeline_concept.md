# Data Pipeline Concept

1. Sample two polarizations at the selected bandwidth and quantization.
2. Channelize or record raw baseband according to the observing mode.
3. Time-tag data against the selected clock architecture.
4. Store locally with health and calibration metadata.
5. Transfer by docking or crosslink.
6. Correlate onboard Pez, in a distributed cluster, or on the ground.
7. Downlink visibilities, calibration data, and selected raw segments.
8. Reconstruct images/light curves and estimate science parameters.

The Month-2 data budget must quantify every arrow in this flow.
