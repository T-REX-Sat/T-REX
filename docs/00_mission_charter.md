# Mission Charter

## Mission proposition

T-REX is a time-domain space-VLBI observatory intended to observe black holes and compact radio sources as evolving systems rather than static images. The source concept combines deployable DiskSat radio telescopes, a central Pez bus, local VLBI recording, rendezvous/docking, onboard or centralized correlation, and high-rate downlink.

## Primary science themes

1. Time-resolve Sgr A* and M87* horizon-scale variability.
2. Constrain black-hole spin and distinguish accretion states through variability and polarimetric monitoring.
3. Resolve or monitor candidate supermassive black-hole binaries.
4. Conduct rapid-response, time-domain millimeter observations of AGN jets and transients.

## Six-month mission

The next six months are not a spacecraft development phase. They are a **concept convergence phase** designed to establish whether a scientifically valuable and technically credible initial T-REX mission can fit a Pioneers-class pathfinder, requires a SMEX-class mission, or should be framed as a larger strategic architecture with smaller precursor demonstrations.

## Success statement for January 2027

The team can present one controlled reference mission and one lower-cost pathfinder, explain their science return, reproduce every key performance number, identify the top five technology gaps, and show a credible funding and maturation path.
