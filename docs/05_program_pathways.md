# Program and Funding Pathways

## ASTRA

ASTRA is an Astrophysics Division initiative for identifying and developing early-stage strategic mission concepts, studying them at low fidelity, and advancing them ahead of formal pre-Phase A. It is best used to mature the **strategic T-REX architecture**, not to claim that the full concept already fits a small-mission cost cap.

## NIAC

NIAC supports visionary, technically credible advanced concepts through staged studies. The strongest T-REX fit is a narrowly defined enabling innovation, for example:

- autonomous assembly of a precision millimeter-wave aperture;
- reusable deployable reflector segments;
- precision time synchronization without a conventional maser on every element;
- Pez-based servicing, recovery, and reconfiguration;
- low-thrust deployment of a reconfigurable VLBI array.

## Flight Opportunities and TechLeap

Flight Opportunities provides access to commercial suborbital and hosted orbital testing. TechLeap adds challenge-based funding and flight opportunities for flight-ready payloads. The 2026 Robotically Manipulated Payload Challenge directly overlaps with modular assembly, robotic manipulation, sensor deployment, and reconfiguration. A T-REX entry should be a self-contained demonstration, not an entire observatory.

## Astrophysics Pioneers

Pioneers supports compelling astrophysics using smaller hardware with a $20M cost cap. The full reconfigurable constellation is unlikely to fit without strong descope and evidence. A pathfinder could focus on one decisive science measurement plus one enabling technology.

## SMEX

The 2026 Astrophysics SMEX cost cap is $190M. This is the appropriate comparison class for a more complete initial observatory, but the concept would still need strong heritage, a credible team, cost reserves, and a tightly bounded mission design.

## Recommended portfolio

Run three linked tracks:

1. **Strategic architecture:** ASTRA and community engagement.
2. **Technology maturation:** NIAC plus Flight Opportunities/TechLeap.
3. **Flight mission:** Pioneers pathfinder leading toward a SMEX-class observatory.
