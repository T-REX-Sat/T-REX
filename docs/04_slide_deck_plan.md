# Six-Slide Stakeholder Deck Plan

## Slide 1 - SWaP-C and mission-class requirements

Show the reference spacecraft, payload allocations, and two cost-class columns:

- Pioneers-class pathfinder: a reduced science/technology mission under the current $20M program cap.
- SMEX-class observatory: a more complete mission concept under the 2026 $190M AO cap.

The slide should state which functions are descoped in the pathfinder.

## Slide 2 - Critical mission parameters

Use a compact table with formula, current assumption, preliminary result, target, and top uncertainty for:

- SEFD;
- baseline thermal noise;
- ultra-stable oscillator/time-transfer requirement;
- raw and processed data payload;
- angular resolution;
- temporal resolution;
- orbital configuration;
- coherence time.

Do not show a number without an assumption ID.

## Slide 3 - Preliminary Concept of Operations

Use a left-to-right lifecycle graphic: launch, spiral/transfer, deploy, calibrate, observe, record, rendezvous/crosslink, correlate, downlink, redeploy.

## Slide 4 - Nominal timeline

Show the six-month concept-maturation plan plus a notional longer pathway: concept study, breadboard, flight demonstration, pathfinder, science mission.

## Slide 5 - Funding and program opportunities

Place programs by purpose rather than prestige:

- ASTRA: strategic concept maturation.
- NIAC: breakthrough technology study.
- Flight Opportunities/TechLeap: focused flight demonstration.
- Pioneers: low-cost astrophysics pathfinder.
- SMEX: more complete competed science mission.

## Slide 6 - Brown student opportunities

Map work packages to outcomes: credit, UTRA/SPRINT, posters, conference abstracts, papers, patents/invention disclosures, hardware, software, proposal sections, and stakeholder presentations.
