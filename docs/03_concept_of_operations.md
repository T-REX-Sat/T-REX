# Preliminary Concept of Operations

1. Launch Pez and stowed DiskSats into a parking orbit.
2. Commission Pez power, communications, navigation, timing, and propulsion.
3. Deploy and commission DiskSats sequentially.
4. Deploy or unfold reflectors and validate surface/pointing performance.
5. Establish inter-satellite timing and orbit-determination links.
6. Conduct observations in one of three modes:
   - dispersed space-VLBI;
   - mixed space-ground VLBI;
   - combined or clustered sensitivity mode, if supported by the selected architecture.
7. Record raw or channelized data locally.
8. Transfer data to Pez by rendezvous/docking or high-rate crosslink.
9. Correlate onboard, partially process onboard, or downlink selected data products according to the data budget.
10. Reconfigure the constellation and repeat observations.
11. Execute secondary science and technology demonstrations.

## Low-thrust transfer option

A high-apogee variant may use a SMART-1-like solar-electric spiral rather than direct chemical injection. The attraction is high specific impulse and reduced propellant mass; the costs are transfer duration, power demand, operational complexity, repeated radiation-belt exposure, and delayed science. The six-month study must compare this option against direct injection and mixed propulsion using a common mass, power, schedule, and radiation model.
