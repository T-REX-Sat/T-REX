# Governance and Reviews

## Leadership

- Mission Lead: science vision, external representation, final decisions.
- Systems Integration Lead: requirements, interfaces, assumptions, reviews.
- Hardware Lead.
- Software Lead.
- Theory Lead.
- Proposal Lead.
- Student Program Coordinator.

## Decision process

1. Open an issue describing the question.
2. Identify affected requirements and assumptions.
3. Assign owners from at least two workstreams.
4. Produce a trade table with evidence.
5. Record the decision in `docs/decisions/ADR-XXX-title.md`.
6. Update requirements, risks, calculations, and slides.

## Monthly reviews

- M1 Baseline Definition Review.
- M2 Science and System Requirements Review.
- M3 Architecture Trade Review.
- M4 Technology Demonstration Review.
- M5 Proposal Red-Team Review.
- M6 Concept Maturity Review.

## Configuration control

The following require formal approval: constellation size, frequency band, reflector geometry, orbit family, timing architecture, correlation location, mission class, and science threshold changes.
