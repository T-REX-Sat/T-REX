# Theoretical Development

The theory team converts broad objectives into measurable observables and source-specific requirements.

## Required analyses

- correlated flux, size, variability, and polarization assumptions for each target;
- visibility amplitudes versus baseline and frequency;
- cadence needed to distinguish MAD and SANE variability;
- SMBHB angular separation and flux distributions;
- scattering/refractive noise and calibration effects;
- end-to-end parameter-estimation demonstrations.
